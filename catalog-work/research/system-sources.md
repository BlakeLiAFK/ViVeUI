## Version and reversibility findings

- Advanced replaces For developers in 25H2; sudo requires 24H2+.
- Dev Drive documents build 22621.2338+, admin, 8 GB RAM and 50 GB free space; no in-place conversion of existing volumes.
- Windows repair via Update requires 22H2 and the February 2024 optional update or later; managed update devices may be excluded.
- Smart App Control documentation restricts fresh installations and returning to evaluation; not modeled as freely reversible.
- Removed local location-history settings were rejected; current per-app access and fallback location remain.
- Win32 long paths still require application opt-in; Group Policy availability depends on edition.
- Windows Sandbox needs supported edition/virtualization; ARM64 begins at 22H2. WSL mirrored networking needs 22H2+ and compatible WSL.
- Deletion, reset, quarantine, encryption, executed elevated commands and previously exposed data do not have generic one-click undo.
